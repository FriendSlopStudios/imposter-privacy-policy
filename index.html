🛡️ Overview & Core Principles

FriendSlop Studios publishes Imposter: Infiltrator Game, an offline-first local pass-and-play social deduction game designed for players aged 13 and older.

Key Privacy Principle: We do not operate a user account system, track player identities across apps, or receive your local game words, player names, or friend groups onto our servers.

📱 1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app maintains strictly local data on your device using Android DataStore and Room Database:

  • Age Band Selection: Under 13, 13–17, or 18+. We never request or store exact birth dates. Under-13 users are blocked from proceeding.
  • Game Configuration & Preferences: Dark mode, sound effects toggle, haptic feedback toggle, and discussion timer preferences.
  • Credits & Timed Unlocks: Earned credits balance and timestamps for temporary (15-minute) category/modifier unlocks.
  • Custom Game Content: Custom word packs and saved friend group player lists created by you.
  • Session Recovery: Active round state so a game is not lost if the app is momentarily backgrounded.

Note: Player names and custom prompts are entered locally by participants sharing the device. Avoid inputting real personal, financial, or sensitive information. Android cloud backup is explicitly disabled for local app state.

📢 2. Google Consent & Advertising (Non-Premium)

For players using the free tier, third-party advertising is supplied via Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and managed by the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP):

  • Consent Verification: Google UMP requests privacy and tracking choices before Google Mobile Ads is initialized or any ad request is sent.
  • Technical Data Processed by Google: Device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device model/OS version, consent signals, ad impressions, user interactions, and anti-fraud signals under Google's policies.
  • Teen & Minor Protection (Ages 13–17): The app requests Google's designated teen treatment, a maximum ad content rating of T (Teen), and restricted/non-personalized ad handling.
  • Under-13 Protection: Users selecting the under-13 age band are blocked before any ad SDK or Google service is initialized.

💳 3. Google Play In-App Billing

The optional Premium Lifetime Unlock is processed entirely by Google Play Billing:

  • Google manages financial transactions, credit cards, billing addresses, and payment accounts.
  • The app receives only cryptographic purchase tokens, entitlement verification, localized price strings, and order acknowledgement status.
  • No credit card, bank account, or payment credentials ever touch the app or our servers.

🔒 4. Permissions & Hardware Access

The app is built with strict permission minimization:

  • NO Camera or Video Recording permissions.
  • NO Microphone or Audio Recording permissions.
  • NO Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS access.
  • NO Precise GPS location permissions.
  • NO External Storage / Photo library access.
  • NO Cleartext (unencrypted HTTP) network traffic permitted.

📊 5. Analytics & Diagnostics

The app does not contain Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Facebook SDK, or any proprietary telemetry trackers. Google Play may provide anonymized, aggregated crash and ANR reports via the Google Play Console (Play Vitals) as part of standard Android distribution.

🗑️ 6. Data Retention, Control & Deletion

You have full control over your on-device data:

  • In-App Wipe: Open Settings → Erase All Local Data to instantly purge all stored custom categories, friend groups, credits, and preferences.
  • Age Band Reset: Reset your age gate answer at any time via Settings → Reset Age Selection.
  • System Wipe: Clearing App Data in Android OS Settings or uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data.
  • Ad Choices: You can review or modify your Google privacy choices anytime via Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.

👶 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

The game is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If an under-13 age selection is made, the application immediately halts and prevents access to gameplay, ads, and in-app purchases. If you believe a child has provided information through Google services, please utilize Google's parental tools and contact us.

⚖️ 8. Regional Rights & Contact

Under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and applicable state/international privacy laws, you have rights regarding access, deletion, and consent withdrawal. Because all game content is local to your device, use the in-app wipe features. For developer inquiries or questions about this policy, reach us at:
