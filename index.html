🔒 Privacy Policy

The Imposter — by Friendslop Studios

Effective Date: August 20, 2026

Friendslop Studios ("we," "our," or "us") built The Imposter as a free, ad-supported application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our app.

By using The Imposter, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

The Imposter uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect the following information:

  • Device identifiers (including Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (used to infer approximate/coarse location for localized ads)
  • Device information (model, operating system version, screen size)
  • App interaction data and crash diagnostics
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use their services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services.

b) Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Imposter stores the following data locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party:

  • Game preferences (dark mode, sound, haptics settings)
  • Unlocked category progress and credits
  • Custom word categories you create
  • Friend group names and members

c) Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit: your name, email address, or any personal contact information; location data (GPS or precise location); photos, camera, or microphone data; contacts, call logs, or SMS; any account or login credentials.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Google AdMob uses collected data to serve relevant advertisements, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud.
  • Locally stored preferences are used solely to remember your settings and game progress between sessions.

3. Third-Party Services

Google AdMob (Google LLC)

Purpose: Displaying advertisements

Data collected: Device identifiers, IP address, app usage data, ad interaction data

Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not use any other third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's own privacy policy and data practices.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Locally stored data: You can delete all app data at any time through your device's Settings > Apps > The Imposter > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • AdMob data: Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's data retention policies. You can manage your Google advertising preferences at adssettings.google.com and reset your Advertising ID in your device settings.

6. Your Rights

a) For Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland (GDPR)

You have the right to: access the personal data processed about you; request correction or deletion of your data; object to or restrict processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time.

For data processed by Google AdMob, you can exercise these rights through Google's privacy tools or by contacting Google directly.

b) For Users in California (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to: know what personal information is collected; request deletion; opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information; and non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We do not sell personal information. To opt out of personalized advertising, adjust your device's ad settings or use the Google consent dialog provided in the app.

7. Children's Privacy

The Imposter is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Ad Consent

The Imposter uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain your consent for personalized advertising where required by law (e.g., in the European Economic Area). You can change your ad consent preferences at any time through the app's settings.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information associated with the app. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
